02Coach Brian

A career
across the game.

Brian is NESA’s Director of Coaching. He has coached for over 30 years at the youth, high school and college levels, and with the Rochester Rhinos.

As a player, Brian played club, high school (Lewiston-Porter High School), college soccer (Plattsburgh State) and in the Canadian Professional Soccer League at London City.

That path — from player, to coach, to goalkeeper specialist, to Director of Coaching — is the experience every NESA session is built on.
